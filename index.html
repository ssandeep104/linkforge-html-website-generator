How does Linkforge work?
§01 — Input

Paste raw HTML or upload .html page source files from creator channels, photo sets, bookmarks, or social feeds.

Choose from six responsive layout templates. Untick links you don't want, customize names, titles, and media selections.

Download a single, standalone HTML page containing your curated, source-grouped watchlist or gallery to host anywhere.
